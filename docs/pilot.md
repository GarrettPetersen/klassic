# First episode: The Personality Market

Use **Erich Fromm on The Mike Wallace Interview, May 25, 1958**. The [Harry Ransom Center](https://hrc.contentdm.oclc.org/digital/collection/p15878coll90/id/64/rec/26) supplies the complete 4,441-word transcript. It was retrieved successfully on September 9, 2026, including opening and closing announcements. This expands the original host list to another contemporary interview program.

Select the continuous exchange about selling one's personality, ending before the transition to social relationships. Fromm distinguishes manual work from work requiring a marketable personality, then connects market demand with self-worth. Preserve that distinction and his qualifications.

## Edit treatment

Working title: **The Personality Market**. Krusty earnestly examining the sale of personality supplies the joke. Avoid extra gag dialogue. Open in a two-shot, cut to the guest for the definition, use a short Krusty listening sip, and return to the guest for the closing analogy. End without a punchline.
